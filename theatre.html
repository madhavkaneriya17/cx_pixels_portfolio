A New Stage for Student Living in Piraeus

Experience premium, all-inclusive student living in the cultural hub of Piraeus. Pixals Theatro is architecturally inspired by local theatrical heritage, offering beautifully designed private suites alongside active community spaces. Enjoy 24/7 access to our rooftop terrace with panoramic sea views, private cinema room, modern fitness center, and co-working study lounges.
