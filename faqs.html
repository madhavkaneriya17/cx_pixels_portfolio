About pixals

All-inclusive living at Units means your rent covers all utility bills (electricity, water, high-speed Wi-Fi), fully furnished apartments, maintenance services, access to community spaces, and 24/7 security with zero hidden costs.

Residents enjoy private study lounges, rooftop terraces, fitness centers, cinema and gaming rooms, laundry facilities, bicycle storage, and communal social kitchens.

Buying a pixals

You can contact our sales and investment team directly through the contact page or booking form to receive the available unit inventory, floor plans, and investment brochure.

Standard documentation includes a valid government-issued ID or passport, proof of address, tax identification number (AFM/TIN), and proof of funds.

Selling a pixals

Our dedicated asset management and sales division can handle the entire resale process for you, connecting with qualified buyers from our global network.

Rental terms

Cancellations made within the grace period specified in your booking agreement are eligible for a full or partial refund according to our standardized cancellation policy.

Managing your pixals

We have an on-site building management and technical team that handles daily maintenance, housekeeping of common areas, and quick resolution of service tickets.

Residents can report any maintenance issue through our digital resident portal or by notifying reception, and our technicians address urgent issues within 24 hours.
