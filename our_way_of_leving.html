Hello, this is MD. Welcome to my site

A new approach to student accommodation.

Designed around people and their everyday lives. With spaces that have a sense of privacy and at the same time a sense of belonging.

Units is not just accommodation; it is a complete lifestyle in a sustainable, community-first environment that enhances the student experience.

We believe student living should feel natural, not temporary. Functional, but also personal. Social, yet never forced. That’s why we design spaces that support everyday life, create room for connection, and take the hassle out of it. Because living shouldn’t feel like a compromise. It should feel just right.
What defines us?

Your Unit

Your own private space, designed for comfort, focus and everyday living.

Community living spaces

Spaces that inspire without holding you back – for study, relaxation, workouts, and connection.

Shared Amenities

Open access 24/7 to state-of-the-art gym, laundry rooms, and lounge areas.

Study & Work Hubs

Quiet study corners and collaborative tables built for focused academic success.

24/7 Security & Support

Smart digital access control, CCTV monitoring, and night patrols for your peace of mind.

Vibrant Community

Regular events, workshops, and social meet-ups to connect with fellow students from day one.

What defines us?

For People

Everything starts with how it feels to live here. From private spaces to shared experiences, people always come first.

By Design

Nothing is accidental. Every detail is designed to support the way you live and the way you feel, every day.

With Care

At the heart of everything we do. We care for the people who live here, the spaces we create, and the city we’re part of.

Why it's different

Because it’s shaped around real everyday life.

Because it’s more than just a place to stay.

Because feeling at home is what matters most.
